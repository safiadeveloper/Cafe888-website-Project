Where we began

Good beans, a short menu, and enough room to sit down.

Kettle & Fig opened in a rented corner with a single counter and four stools. We kept it simple from day one: good beans, a short menu done properly, and enough room to actually sit down.

Every plate is built around what's in season. The bakes come out of our own oven each morning. Nothing on the menu is reheated to order — if it's not fresh, it's not served.

It's grown into a full room now, but the corner-cafe habits stayed: we still know most people's order before they sit down.
