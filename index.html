Our story

Started with one espresso machine and a rented corner.

Kettle & Fig opened as a single counter and four stools. We kept it simple: good beans, a short menu done properly, and enough room to actually sit down.

Every plate is built around what's in season. The bakes come out of our own oven each morning — nothing is reheated to order.
